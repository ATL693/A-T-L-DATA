Terms of Service

1. The service. A T L DATA lets you buy mobile data, airtime, and (where available) bills for Nigerian networks, paid for from a wallet you fund yourself.

2. Wallet funds. Your wallet balance has no value outside this platform and cannot be withdrawn as cash. It may only be used to pay for services on A T L DATA.

3. Pricing. Prices are set by our network partners and may change at any time without notice. The price shown at the time of purchase is the price charged.

4. Failed transactions. If a purchase fails on our end or our provider's end, the amount is automatically returned to your wallet. If a purchase shows successful but you did not receive the data/airtime, contact support with your transaction reference.

5. Your PIN. Your transaction PIN is private. You are responsible for any purchase authorized with your correct PIN. Contact support immediately if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.

6. Prohibited use. You may not use A T L DATA for fraud, to resell services in violation of network terms, or for any unlawful purpose. We may suspend accounts that show signs of abuse.

7. Liability. We work to keep the service reliable but cannot guarantee uninterrupted access, since purchases depend on third-party network and payment providers.

8. Changes. These terms may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the platform means you accept the current terms.

Privacy Policy

Information we collect. Your name, email, phone number, and transaction history, collected when you register and use the platform.

How we use it. To create and secure your account, process your purchases and deposits, and provide customer support.

Who we share it with. We share only what's necessary to complete your purchases with our service providers: Google Firebase (authentication and data storage), our data/airtime network partner, and our payment partner for wallet funding. We do not sell your information to anyone.

Your transaction PIN is never stored as entered — only a one-way cryptographic hash of it is kept, which cannot be reversed back into your PIN.

Your rights. You can ask us to correct your account details at any time from My Account, or contact support to request deletion of your account.

Contact. For any privacy question, reach us through the Support page in the app.
