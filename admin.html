Sales & Profit Overview

Revenue is based on successful sales transactions. Profit is only calculated when the transaction contains cost/profit data; missing cost data is not guessed.

Note: transaction history is limited to the latest 300 records. For accurate accounting, ensure your backend records provider cost or profit for each sale.
